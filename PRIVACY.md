# Marker Studio Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

Marker Studio adds visual effects to webpages for screen recordings. The extension processes data on your device and sends no data to the developer or any third party.

## What Marker Studio uses

- **Page content.** When you queue a highlight, Marker Studio keeps a temporary reference to the selected text range on the current page. Its other tools use page layout and elements to zoom, scroll, hide fixed or sticky elements, and visually blur elements.
- **Input for page tools.** Marker Studio checks keyboard events for its shortcuts, tracks the page element under the pointer for blur, and uses pointer or scroll positions to position effects. It keeps no record of your keystrokes, pointer activity, or browsing history.
- **Preferences.** Marker Studio saves your highlight color, opacity, speed, and theme, zoom settings, scroll speed, and blur amount in Chrome's local extension storage. It does not sync those preferences or store selected text there.

## How long data is kept

The current tab holds selections and effects temporarily. Press Esc to clear the effects and queued selections. Reloading or closing the page clears this tab state. The popup's **Defaults** button replaces your saved settings with the defaults. Uninstalling the extension removes its local settings.

## Sharing and use

Marker Studio has no account, analytics, advertising, or remote service. It does not record your screen, sell user data, use it for unrelated purposes or lending decisions, or transfer it off your device. Marker Studio's use of data complies with the Chrome Web Store's [Limited Use requirements](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/limited-use).

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the project through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/brkunver/marker-studio/issues). Use the contact email on the Chrome Web Store listing for questions that involve private information.
